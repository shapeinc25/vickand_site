How we got here

My UX/UI journey began over six years ago in Web3. Not exactly the easiest place to start, but I didn’t plan it that way. I joined a team and found myself designing experiences for technology that most people were only beginning to understand.

Over the years, I went from designing interfaces to leading design teams, shaping product strategies, and managing products from concept to launch. Somewhere along the way, I started exploring my own ideas. That’s how Vickand came to life.

Today, we’re a team of two — a Product Manager and a Product Designer — creating our own apps and games while sharing our expertise with indie developers, vibe coders, and small teams.

Often, good UX isn’t about starting over. It’s about removing friction, making interactions feel familiar, and giving your product a consistent identity that people recognize and remember.
