## Users

Visitors choosing an independent app or game, and developers or product teams looking for UX and design help. These audiences and the two main visitor paths are explicit in the owner-supplied HTML.

## Product Purpose

Present Vickand’s four products and its UX/design services clearly, and let a visitor contact the team or request free initial scoping. Paid work starts only after a separate scope and price agreement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static, crawlable EN/RU/UK pages with clean URLs; shared layouts and source modules; real delivery/error states for contact, support and release-news interest requests; separate app policy links. No site checkout or visitor account. Release-news submissions are requests sent to the owner, not verified marketing-list enrollment. No visitor email sends or campaigns are authorized in this task.

## Brand Commitments

The supplied vickand-site_100_90.html is the visual and content authority. Reuse matching handoff assets and working modules. Preserve the Apps & Games light surface and UX & Design dark surface, their animations and mini games. Use the owner’s 4 logical-pixel action icon/label spacing preference, preserving explicitly authored brand navigation geometry.

No fabricated subscribers, ratings, customers, testimonials or results. ## Product Principles

- A supplied visual reference outranks a replacement design concept.
- Source structure and server-rendered language content must remain maintainable.
- Forms confirm only an actual accepted server response.
- Data use, marketing consent and legal descriptions must match implementation.
- Optimize measured loading and motion costs without sacrificing content or accessibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, meaningful headings and link destinations, no-JavaScript reading, mobile layouts and reduced-motion support are required. Test English, Russian and Ukrainian at phone and desktop sizes.
